Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are, which ones nesttechsupport.com uses, and how you can manage your preferences. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, and to provide information to the site owner. We also use your browser's local storage to remember your cookie preference, which works in a similar way to a cookie.

2. How we use cookies

We only load non-essential cookies, such as Google Analytics, once you have given consent via the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time using the "Manage Cookies" link in the site footer.

nesttech_cookie_consent NestTech Support (stored in your browser's local storage, not a cookie) Remembers your cookie consent choice Strictly necessary Until you clear your browser data
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique visitors Analytics (consent-based) Up to 2 years

3. Other third-party services

Our Site loads fonts directly from Google Fonts to display its typography. This is not a cookie, but it does mean your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address when the Site loads. See Google's own privacy policy for details of how they handle this data.

4. Managing your preferences

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies at any time via the cookie banner shown when you first visit the Site, or by selecting "Manage Cookies" in the footer of any page. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings — see aboutcookies.org for guidance across different browsers. Blocking all cookies may affect how parts of the Site function.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
