FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting in touch.

How quickly can you respond to a call-out?

For remote support I can often connect within the hour. On-site visits across London are typically booked within 24–48 hours, with same-day availability depending on schedule. Call 0203 488 1396 for urgent issues.

What areas of London do you cover?

The whole of Greater London — including North, South, East and West London. Areas regularly covered include Hackney, Brixton, Clapham, Dulwich, Canary Wharf, Islington, Shoreditch, Wimbledon and more. Remote support is available UK-wide.

How much does IT support cost?

Pricing depends on the job. Remote sessions start from £45 and on-site visits start from £75. A free quote is always available before any work begins — no hidden charges, no call-out surprise fees.

Do you support both home users and businesses?

Yes — NestTech Support works with home users, remote workers, home offices, and small-to-medium businesses. Whether it's a single laptop or a full office network, the same personal service applies.

What qualifications do you hold?

CCNA (Cisco Certified Network Associate), CCNP (Cisco Certified Network Professional), and Cyber Essentials certified. This means your network, data and devices are in qualified hands.
