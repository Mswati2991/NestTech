FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting in touch.

How quickly can you respond?

For remote support I can often connect within the hour. On-site visits are typically booked within 24–48 hours, with same-day availability depending on schedule. Call 0203 488 1396 for urgent issues.

What areas of London do you cover?

The whole of Greater London — Hackney, Brixton, Clapham, Dulwich, Canary Wharf, Islington, Shoreditch, Wimbledon and more. Remote support is available UK-wide.

How much does IT support cost?

Remote sessions start from £45 and on-site visits from £75. A free quote is always available before any work begins — no hidden charges.

What qualifications do you hold?

CCNA, CCNP (both Cisco Certified), and Cyber Essentials certified. Your network, data and devices are in qualified hands.
